# KTLauncher — Offline Edition

A full-featured offline Minecraft launcher (Offline Mode) .

---

## 🚀 Direct Launch

All you have to do is double-click directly on the launcher file:  
👉 `ktlauncher.exe`

---

## 🎨 Interface & Appearance

- **3D Cinematic Background:** Inspired by futuristic voxel worlds (cyan, purple, and gold neon beams).
- **Sleek Top Bar:** Features the `KTLAUNCHER` logo with a glowing `OFFLINE` badge.
- **Eye-Friendly Cards & Elements:** Dark Acrylic Glassmorphism design that smoothly and seamlessly highlights content and controls.

---

## 👤 Account Creation

1. Open `ktlauncher.exe`.
2. On the login screen or from the bottom menu, click **Add Account**.
3. Type any username you want (e.g., `Alex`, or any name you choose).
4. Click the **Login/Create** button.
5. The account will be created and selected immediately so you can play offline without needing a Microsoft account or an external connection.

---

## 📁 Transferring Accounts & Backup

All your accounts, username settings, and IDs (UUIDs) are automatically and portably saved/updated in this file located in the same launcher folder:  
📄 `ktlauncher-accounts.json`

### How to transfer your account to another device?

1. Copy the `ktlauncher-accounts.json` file.
2. Place it in the `KTLauncher` folder on any new device, or share it with your friends.
3. As soon as you run the launcher, your accounts and current username will appear immediately without needing to re-enter them!
